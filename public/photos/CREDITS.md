## A note on the photos themselves

None of these show Noisette's actual products. `dubai.jpg` is a chocolate bar,
not a kunafa-filled one. `piscok.jpg` is a plain croissant. They are the right
shape and mood for laying out the page, and they are wrong in the details. Treat
the vitrine as unshot until a real shoot replaces these.
